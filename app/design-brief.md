# Apprenti Live Tracker
Audience: UK sixth-form students seeking apprenticeships, aged 16–18.
Concept: An application pulse desk, immediate answers and actionable signals.
Delivery tier: editorial, responsive, minimal overhead.
Animation mode: non-animated — user specifically requests clean, ultra-fast utility with no decorative distractions.
Brand: white #FFFFFF, slate #F8FAFC, charcoal #0F172A, blue #2563EB; green #10B981, amber #F59E0B, red #EF4444 statuses.
Type: DM Sans and Space Mono as practical alternatives to Inter / JetBrains Mono.
Structure: nav; hero with sample intake panel; searchable interactive tracker; three value cards; founder; footer; modal and side drawer.
Interactive moments: multi-filter table, persistent saves, app stage drawer and fast tip submission.
Data boundary: Seed listings unverified and clearly labelled; browser-only updates are not passed off as aggregated community intel.
No paywall, login, corporate sales or waitlist.
